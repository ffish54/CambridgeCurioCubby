PRIVACY

Your details

Cambridge Curio Cubbyhole is a family shop in Wisbech. This page says what we keep when you make an offer on a piece, and why.

Offers

If you use Make an offer on a piece, we store your name, your email, your phone number if you give one, the piece, the option you chose, the amount, and any message. We keep them so we can reply about that offer. We do not show offers on the public shop, and we do not sell the details on.

The website does not take payment. An accepted offer is settled with us directly, by the arrangement we write back about.

Enquiries

Enquire about this piece opens an email in your own mail program to curiocubbyhole@gmail.com. That message is not stored by the website.

Asking about your details

Write to curiocubbyhole@gmail.com if you would like a copy of an offer you sent, or if you would like us to delete it.
