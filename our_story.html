WELCOME TO THE CUBBYHOLE

Where old treasures
find new stories.

Welcome to Cambridge Curio Cubbyhole, where every item has a story to tell.

Cambridge Curio Cubbyhole began with something very simple — a love of collecting and a family full of cherished memories.

Our Nanny has always had a wonderful eye for unusual and beautiful things. Over the years, she loved collecting all sorts of vintage treasures, from glassware and ceramics to ornaments, collectables and all manner of curious little finds.

Her home became a place filled with pieces from different times, each one with its own character and story.

A LIFETIME OF TREASURES
THE BEGINNING

CHAPTER ONE

The Beginning

◆

As her collection grew, so did our appreciation for the wonderful things she had gathered.

What began as a love of finding and collecting vintage pieces eventually became the inspiration for Cambridge Curio Cubbyhole.

Each cupboard, shelf and corner seemed to contain another little treasure waiting to be discovered.

"A lifetime of collecting can become a lifetime of memories."

CHAPTER TWO

A New Chapter

Sadly, our family was faced with a difficult change when our Nanny was diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease.

As her condition progressed, some of us made the decision to leave our careers and dedicate our time to caring for her.

While this has been a challenging journey for our family, it has also given us the opportunity to spend precious time together and care for someone who has always meant so much to us.

The shop began as a way of helping our family manage this change.

CHAPTER THREE

The Hunt Continues

Today, Cambridge Curio Cubbyhole continues to grow beyond our Nanny's original collection.

We regularly search for interesting vintage pieces, curiosities and collectables that we think deserve to be appreciated again.

We never quite know what we'll find.
